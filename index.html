About Dr. Olive Lim-Aites

Dr. Olive Lim-Aites leads the clinic with a strong commitment to excellence in modern, family-friendly dentistry. Known for her gentle touch and compassionate care, she ensures that every patient—from young children to adults—feels comfortable, valued, and well cared for.

With a focus on personalized, high-quality treatments, Dr. Lim-Aites creates a warm and welcoming environment where families can achieve healthy, confident smiles together.
